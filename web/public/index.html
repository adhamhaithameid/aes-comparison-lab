Quick Facts About AES

Standardized

November 2001, by NIST (FIPS 197)

Key Sizes

128, 192, or 256 bits

Block Size

Always 128 bits (16 bytes)

Rounds

10, 12, or 14 depending on key size

Usage

TLS, Wi-Fi (WPA2/3), disk encryption, VPNs

Origin

Rijndael cipher by Daemen & Rijmen
